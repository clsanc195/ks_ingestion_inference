# Acme Corporation

Acme Corporation is a robotics company headquartered in Basel. It was founded in 2019 by Elena Vogel.

Elena Vogel serves as the chief executive officer of Acme Corporation. Before founding Acme, she led the automation division at Helvetia Machines.

## Products

Acme's flagship product is the GripperOne, a robotic arm designed for warehouse automation. The GripperOne entered production in 2023.
