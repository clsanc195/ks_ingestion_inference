# The Credit Suisse collapse and UBS takeover

In July 2022, Ulrich Körner replaced Thomas Gottstein as chief executive officer of Credit Suisse.

On 19 March 2023, UBS announced an agreement to acquire Credit Suisse for 3.25 billion US dollars in an emergency rescue deal brokered by Swiss government authorities. The acquisition of Credit Suisse by UBS was completed in June 2023.

In April 2023, Sergio Ermotti replaced Ralph Hamers as chief executive officer of UBS, returning to lead the integration of Credit Suisse.

Axel Lehmann served as chairman of Credit Suisse until June 2023. By 31 May 2024, Credit Suisse ceased to exist as a separate entity following its full integration into UBS.
