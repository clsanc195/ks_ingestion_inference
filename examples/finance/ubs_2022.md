# UBS — company profile (2022)

UBS is a Swiss bank formed on 29 June 1998 through the merger of Union Bank of Switzerland and Swiss Bank Corporation. UBS is headquartered in Zurich, Switzerland.

Ralph Hamers is the chief executive officer of UBS, a position he has held since November 2020. Colm Kelleher became chairman of UBS in April 2022.

UBS operates four main business divisions: Global Wealth Management, Personal & Corporate Banking, Asset Management, and the Investment Bank. UBS is considered the world's largest private bank.
