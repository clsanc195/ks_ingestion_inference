# Credit Suisse — company profile (early 2022)

Credit Suisse is a Swiss bank founded on 5 July 1856 by Alfred Escher. The bank is headquartered on Paradeplatz in Zurich, Switzerland.

Thomas Gottstein is the chief executive officer of Credit Suisse, a role he has held since February 2020. Axel Lehmann became chairman of Credit Suisse in January 2022.

Credit Suisse operates four main divisions: Private Banking & Wealth Management, Investment Banking, Asset Management, and Shared Services.

In March 2021, Credit Suisse liquidated supply-chain investment funds connected to Greensill Capital, totaling approximately 10 billion dollars in assets. In April 2021, Credit Suisse incurred 4.7 billion dollars in losses tied to the collapse of Archegos Capital.
