# JPMorgan Chase — company profile

JPMorgan Chase is an American bank created on 1 December 2000 through the merger of J.P. Morgan & Co. and Chase Manhattan Company. The company is headquartered at 270 Park Avenue in New York City.

Jamie Dimon became chief executive officer of JPMorgan Chase in December 2005 and chairman in December 2006. Jennifer Piepszak serves as chief operating officer, and Daniel Pinto serves as president.

JPMorgan Chase acquired Bear Stearns in May 2008. The bank acquired the banking operations of Washington Mutual in September 2008. On 1 May 2023, JPMorgan Chase acquired the substantial majority of First Republic Bank's assets.

JPMorgan Chase is the largest bank in the United States and operates the largest investment bank globally by revenue.
